How the archive works

An evidence-first approach for reporters, community groups, public agencies, and researchers.

01 / COLLECT

Preserve the source

Link each record to its official publication or retain the original PDF. Capture the issuing agency, document date, and request tracking information.

02 / REVIEW

Separate fact from inference

Identify whether a record is routine oversight, an allegation, a verified finding, or an emergency decision. Remove sensitive personal or security details before publication.

03 / PUBLISH

Make it discoverable

Add neutral summaries and searchable tags. Record corrections and update dates. Original sources remain the primary evidence.
